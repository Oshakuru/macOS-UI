# Light and dark themes

**Decision:** the light theme is built and finished first. Dark follows as a recolour pass plus manual adjustments.

## The catch
Container titles are drawn in a hardcoded dark grey, so they disappear on dark panels. There are two vanilla-safe fixes:

| Option | How | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Lang override | `assets/minecraft/lang/<locale>.json` re-defines title keys with a `§f` (white) prefix | Real white titles | One file per language; renamed containers keep grey text |
| 2. Title strip | Light title well painted behind the title on dark panels | Works for every language and renamed containers | Slightly less "pure" dark |

Plan: use both. Decided during the dark pass.

## Reference study: Colourful Containers (light) vs Darkmode Colourful Containers
- **Two complete standalone zips.** About 515 files are shared and 241 of those differ.
- **The dark theme is a mechanical palette swap** of the five vanilla greys. Coloured artwork is identical in both themes.

| Role | Light (vanilla) | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Slot well | `#8B8B8B` | `#333333` |
| Panel | `#C6C6C6` | `#535353` |
| Bevel highlight | `#FFFFFF` | `#6B6B6B` |
| Slot shadow | `#373737` | `#262626` |
| Panel shade | `#555555` | `#333333` |

- **Titles:** both blank most container titles in the lang files. The dark pack adds `§f` to "Inventory" and the creative tab names across 134 languages, which confirms Option 1 works.
- **Not copied:** they depend on mods (OptiGUI, respackopts), hide titles, declare an over-broad format range (0–99), and maintain two hand-kept copies.

## What we adopt
1. **Dark is derived, not redrawn:** a fixed token map (light → dark) applied by `tools/build.py`, plus hand-tuned exceptions.
2. **Two standalone zips from one source.**
3. **Lang overrides generated by script** (title keys only), with the title strip as a fallback.
4. **Controls are shared:** buttons, HUD and tooltips are already graphite in both themes.
